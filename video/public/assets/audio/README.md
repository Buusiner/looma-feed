These are original, locally synthesized instrumental scores for the Looma films.
No third-party samples, recordings, vocals, or commercial songs were used.
Rebuild with `python scripts/score.py` (NumPy required). The complete score and
understated transition effects are baked into each replaceable WAV file.
120 BPM; 48 kHz stereo; normalized to a target of -18 LUFS and -2 dBTP.
